# Community code of conduct

We want Weave discussions to be technically rigorous and welcoming.

- Be respectful, specific, and patient with people at different experience levels.
- Critique code and claims, not people.
- Do not publish another person's credentials, logs, identity, or private traffic.
- Do not pressure maintainers to enable unsafe defaults or evade platform policy.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, and deliberately misleading security
  claims are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation
to protect users and the project. Sensitive incidents should be reported through
the private channel in `SECURITY.md`, not litigated in a public issue.
